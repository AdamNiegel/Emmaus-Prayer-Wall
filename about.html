Why "Emmaus"

A road, two travelers, and a presence they didn't recognize

The walk

On the day of the resurrection, two disciples walked the seven miles from Jerusalem to Emmaus, heavy with grief. A stranger joined them on the road and asked what they were discussing.

The presence

They didn't recognize him at first. He walked with them, listened, and opened the Scriptures — and somewhere along the road, their sorrow began to lift.

And it came to pass, that, while they communed together and reasoned, Jesus himself drew near, and went with them. Luke 24:15, King James Version

The recognition

It was in the simple act of breaking bread together that their eyes were opened. They realized they had not walked alone at all.

And they said one to another, Did not our heart burn within us, while he talked with us by the way? Luke 24:32, King James Version

Our purpose

What Emmaus is for

This site exists so that no one has to carry a hard season by themselves. You share what's heavy; someone else agrees to carry it with you in prayer. That's the whole idea.

Community guidelines

Sharing well, together

  • Be honest, be brief. A few sentences is enough. You don't need to explain everything.
  • Protect others' privacy. Avoid sharing identifying details about someone else without their consent.
  • Stay respectful. This is a space for encouragement, not debate or judgment.
  • Anonymous is always welcome. You never have to share your name to be prayed for.
  • Praying counts. If you tap "Praying" on a request, take a moment to actually mean it.
